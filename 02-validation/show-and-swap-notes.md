# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

I showed Signal v3 without explaining the layout. The first screen displayed the 60% bounce rate, a plain-language takeaway, user quotes, and one recommended action: “Start the focused-view test.” That button opened an editable test plan._____

## What my partner understood (in their words)

“I understood that people are leaving the dashboard because finding something useful takes too much work. You’re suggesting a simpler first screen and giving me a way to plan that test.”_____

## Where they got confused / pushed back

“I wasn’t initially sure whether 60% was live data or the starting baseline. I also expected the button to help me act on a business insight, but it opened a plan to test the dashboard itself. Clicking that doesn’t necessarily mean the dashboard helped me make a better decision.”_____

## What I'll change as a result

Based on this critique, I would make the baseline label more prominent and clarify what the recommended action delivers. I would also test whether users need a direct business action rather than a usability-test plan.

Alongside clicks and bounce, I’ll ask users to explain what decision they made and why. That will help distinguish meaningful action from simply following a prominent button._____
