# Confidence Line Reflection (optional)

> Module 1 · Velocity. Toy vs tool, and where this build moved you on the Confidence Line.

## Toy or tool?

_A toy generates applause; a tool generates a decision. Which did you build, and how do you know?_

I built an early tool with some toy-like qualities. It helps users identify a priority, inspect evidence, and record a next step. However, the data is mocked and the recommendations are predefined. I demonstrated a possible decision flow; I haven’t yet shown that it improves a real user’s decision._____

## Confidence Line placement

_Where on the Confidence Line did Module 1 leave you, and why?_

Module 1 moved me from “I can describe an idea” to “I can build something people can try.” I’m more confident in my ability to prototype quickly, but still uncertain whether I’m solving the right problem. A clickable build makes an assumption testable; it doesn’t validate it._____

## The feedback that landed hardest

_The single piece of Show & Swap feedback that will change how you build next._

The strongest critique was: “The dashboard recommends a solution before proving the cause.” That came from our review, rather than verified partner feedback. It will change my next build: I’ll make observations, hypotheses, and recommended actions clearly distinguishable, so the interface’s confidence matches its evidence._____
