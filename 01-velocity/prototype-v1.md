## The build

- **What I built:** Signal, a clickable dashboard for marketing and growth teams. The first screen surfaces one priority insight, supporting metrics, channel comparisons, and a suggested next step. Users can inspect evidence, filter channels, save an action plan, and export a brief. All analytics data is mocked._____
- **Tool used:** ChatGPT Work
- **Shareable link:**[ ](https://signal-decisions-dipti-k8m2.bob-explorer-4846.chatgpt.site/)_____Access is currently restricted; partner access needs to be enabled before swapping.

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup. Their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they thought it did:** Helped marketing teams identify where visitors drop off and decide what to investigate next._____
- **What surprised them:** The dashboard suggested a landing-page change before establishing why visitors were leaving. Different channels also received nearly identical recommendations._____
- **The gap between what I intended and what they read:**I intended to make analytics easier to understand and act on. The build communicated marketing performance analysis more clearly than the original problem of dashboard abandonment. It also made recommendations with more confidence than the evidence justified. _____
