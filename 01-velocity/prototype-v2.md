## Weakest section (from v1)

_Where does it look like a toy? Where would a VP of Design refuse to open it? Where does the logic feel fake? Pick the one spot that costs you the most credibility._

The “Your next move” recommendation panel costs the most credibility. It suggests changing the landing-page headline and CTA before establishing why visitors leave. Filtering channels changes the numbers but produces almost the same recommendation, making the logic feel scripted._____
